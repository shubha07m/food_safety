Identity and purpose

The West Bengal Food Safety Evidence Tracker is an independent, non-governmental, initially non-commercial educational, academic and public-interest data-research project. Its initial scope is West Bengal, India, with emphasis on Kolkata and surrounding areas. It organizes publicly available reporting into a structured evidence browser. It is not an official register, government database, restaurant blacklist, accusation platform or substitute for official records.

Kolkata Municipal Corporation (KMC), the Government of West Bengal, FSSAI, news publishers, establishments and other parties do not endorse this project. Source references and links do not imply affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement by either party.

What inclusion means

Inclusion means only that a cited public source reported an event. All external statements remain attributable to their respective sources. The project does not independently assert their underlying truth.

Inclusion does not establish guilt, a violation, unsafe food, criminal conduct, legal liability, intent or negligence. An inspection, sample collection, seizure, disposal or notice is not itself a finding of wrongdoing. No recommendation is made to patronize or avoid any establishment.

SOURCE VERIFIED means the cited source was checked and supports the displayed statement. It does not mean the project independently established the underlying real-world event as true. CROSS-SOURCE VERIFIED concerns support in multiple independent sources and is not a legal or scientific determination.

Users should read the original sources, their surrounding context, dates and subsequent updates. Public reporting can be incomplete, inaccurate, disputed, corrected, withdrawn, outdated or superseded. External links may change or become unavailable; continuous availability is not guaranteed.

Counts and derived context

Counts describe source-verifiable records in this tracker, not all inspections conducted by authorities. They are not official totals and must not be interpreted as prevalence, compliance, risk, wrongdoing or group-level behavior. Absence from the tracker does not imply absence of inspection. Different publication access, languages, reporting practices, periods and source coverage create selection bias.

Derived classifications are contextual metadata, not official food-safety categories or findings. Business format and menu context, where supported, say nothing about social identity, ownership identity, safety, fault or relative quality. Unknown values are retained rather than inferred. There is no denominator for population risk estimates or comparisons.

Purpose limits and misuse

The project is not intended to express the author's personal opinion or to build public opinion for or against any establishment, owner category, dietary group, geographic area, community or other group.

Do not infer religion, caste, ethnicity, political affiliation or community from names, menus, cuisine, neighbourhoods or tracker counts. Do not use the tracker for harassment, targeting, discrimination, boycott, retaliation, reputational attacks, communal interpretation or political/social conclusions. It does not invite allegations or voting on businesses.

Third-party commentary, interpretation, screenshots, reposts and sharing are the responsibility of their authors and do not represent the project's position. This attribution policy is not a declaration that the project has no legal responsibility. Sharers should preserve record IDs, source links, dates, verification status and this contextual disclaimer wherever reasonably possible, and check whether a record has subsequently been updated or suspended.

Corrections and record changes

Corrections and disputes are welcomed under CORRECTIONS.md. The owner may suspend or remove material where accuracy, context or publication risk is uncertain. Disputed, withdrawn or changed records leave the public evidence dataset pending review. A minimal status notice may remain at their record URL.

Meaningful historical changes are recorded through revision notes and prior-version hashes rather than silently rewritten. Private audit history can be retained subject to necessity, privacy and lawful removal requirements. Preservation is not a promise to keep harmful or unnecessary material indefinitely, nor a promise that copies in third-party caches can be recalled.

Automated assistance and rights

Automated tools, including language models where enabled, may assist extraction. Model output is not evidence. Factual fields require supporting source material, deterministic checks and maintainer context review. No model determines guilt, liability, food safety, community identity or wrongdoing.
