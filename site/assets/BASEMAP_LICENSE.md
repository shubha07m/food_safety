# Local geographic reference

West Bengal boundary: geoBoundaries gbOpen, India ADM1, revision `9469f09`,
`geoBoundaries-IND-ADM1_simplified.geojson`. Geometry extracted without further
simplification. Historical reference geometry, not an authoritative boundary claim.

Source: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/

Upstream: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/IND/ADM1

Original data attribution: DataMeet India community; Election Commission of India.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 India (CC BY 2.5 IN).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/in/

Reviewed area anchors are separately sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors,
ODbL: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Per-record location_source links
are preserved in the data. Anchors are not establishment addresses.

The basemap makes no network requests. No tiles, geocoding API, tracking, or fonts.
