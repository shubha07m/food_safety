WEST BENGAL / METHODOLOGY

From reporting to evidence.

Every displayed fact needs a source. Every count needs underlying records.

The initial scope is West Bengal, with emphasis on Kolkata and nearby areas. The dataset starts empty. Prototype values and synthetic test fixtures are never treated as production evidence.

A small, inspectable process

  1. Start with configured public-source article URLs.
  2. Retrieve bounded article text and retain minimal evidence spans.
  3. Check source support, field safety, provenance and possible duplicate events.
  4. Keep uncertain records pending. A maintainer reads the original context and attests to every displayed field before V1 publication.
  5. Generate counts from published rows. Select a chart item to inspect its contributing evidence.

Facts and context stay separate

Reported observations, actions, quantities and dates are source-attributed facts. Menu and business-format categories are separately documented derived context, not official food-safety findings. Missing information remains unknown. No identity is inferred from names, menus or neighbourhoods.

Official material is Tier A; established accessible news is Tier B. Other publications need stronger corroboration. Discovery-only snippets, social media and anonymous claims cannot enter public data. SOURCE VERIFIED describes source support, not independent proof of an event. Updated, disputed, withdrawn and superseded records are excluded from public counts while under review.

Automated tools disclosure

Automated tools, including language models where enabled, may assist with extracting structured fields from publicly available source material. Language-model output is not treated as evidence. Published factual fields must remain linked to supporting source material and pass deterministic validation rules. The system does not use language models to determine guilt, legal liability, food safety, community identity, or whether an establishment committed wrongdoing.

LLM calls are disabled by default and require an explicit opt-in and small call budget. No VLM or face recognition is enabled. Human context review remains necessary because a text match cannot establish meaning or correct attribution.

Coverage is a limitation

Counts describe source-verifiable records in this tracker, not all inspections conducted by authorities. They must not be interpreted as prevalence, compliance, risk, wrongdoing, or group-level behavior. The dataset has no population denominator. Language, source access and reporting practices affect coverage.
