WEST BENGAL / METHODOLOGY

From reporting to evidence.

Every displayed fact needs a source. Every count needs underlying records.

The initial scope is West Bengal, with emphasis on Kolkata and nearby areas. The private evaluation dataset contains a reviewed real-source sample. Prototype values and synthetic test fixtures are never treated as evidence.

A small, inspectable process

  1. Start with configured public-source article URLs.
  2. Retrieve bounded article text and retain minimal evidence spans.
  3. Check source support, field safety, provenance and possible duplicate events.
  4. Keep uncertain records pending. A maintainer reads the original context and attests to every displayed field before publication.
  5. Generate counts from published rows. Select a chart item to inspect its contributing evidence.

Facts and context stay separate

Reported observations, actions, quantities and dates are source-attributed facts. Action, establishment, menu and business-format categories are separately documented derived context—not official food-safety findings. Non-default context requires linked evidence, a method, confidence and review. Missing information remains unknown. No identity is inferred from names, menus, cuisine or neighbourhoods.

Official material is Tier A; established accessible news is Tier B. Other publications need stronger corroboration. Discovery-only snippets, social media and anonymous claims cannot enter public data. SOURCE VERIFIED describes source support, not independent proof of an event. Updated, disputed, withdrawn and superseded records are excluded from public counts while under review.

Automated tools disclosure

Automated tools, including language models where enabled, may assist with extracting structured fields. Language-model output is not evidence. Published factual fields remain linked to source material and pass deterministic validation. Models do not determine guilt, liability, food safety, community identity or wrongdoing.

LLM calls are disabled by default and require explicit opt-in and a small call budget. No VLM or face recognition is enabled. Human context review remains necessary.

Coverage is a limitation

Counts describe source-verifiable records in this tracker, not all inspections conducted by authorities. They must not be interpreted as prevalence, compliance, risk, wrongdoing or group-level behavior. Language, source access and reporting practices affect coverage.
