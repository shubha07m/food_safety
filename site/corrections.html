The correction and source-review policy remains available below.

Corrections, source updates and uncertainty are part of the research process.

A correction review identifies the record ID, disputed field, reason and a supporting public source URL. Relevant context must exclude unsupported allegations, harassment, discriminatory material, private documents and unnecessary personal information.

Maintainers may suspend a record while examining its source and record a neutral revision or dispute status. No fixed response time is promised. No submission changes an evidence record automatically.

Source-review expectations

Source review requires a public URL, publisher, publication date if known, a neutral event description and a short evidence span. A source lead is not an accepted record. Anonymous claims, forwards and unsupported screenshots cannot become published evidence.
