VOLUNTEER MAINTAINERS

Help keep FoodPath source-conscious.

Contribute Puja source and venue checks, nearby-food corrections, Bengali/English accessibility, or careful review of West Bengal Food Safety evidence.

Submissions are reviewed as source leads. They never publish directly and cannot be used to submit allegations, target people, or infer social identity.

Useful contributions

Volunteer maintainers can help verify Puja organizers and edition details, improve open food data, review public sources, check evidence spans, identify corrections, support Bengali/English coverage, improve accessible static-site tooling, and document reproducible research methods.

Standards are part of the work

Food Safety Evidence (West Bengal): independent public-source research, not a government database. Inclusion is not a finding of wrongdoing. Puja listings and nearby food are separate from this evidence module.

Every contribution must follow the methodology, correction process, code of conduct and source policy. Use only publicly available source material. Do not send private documents, personal data, unsupported allegations, screenshots without a public source, or material that encourages harassment, boycott, discrimination or communal interpretation.

Know a Puja we are missing? Suggest a Puja through the configured private-response Google Form on the homepage. Food Safety source intake is separate and follows its evidence rules.
