VOLUNTEER MAINTAINERS

Help maintain the evidence carefully.

Contributions are reviewed manually. They never publish automatically and cannot be used to submit allegations, target people, or infer social identity.

Useful contributions

Volunteer maintainers can help review public sources, check evidence spans, identify corrections, support Bengali/English coverage, improve accessible static-site tooling, and document reproducible research methods.

Standards are part of the work

Every contribution must follow the methodology, correction process, code of conduct and source policy. Use only publicly available source material. Do not send private documents, personal data, unsupported allegations, screenshots without a public source, or material that encourages harassment, boycott, discrimination or communal interpretation.

Read contribution guidance on GitHub ↗ Request a correction or suggest a source

A publicly accessible intake channel must be configured by the maintainer before public launch. Until then, this private evaluation repository’s controlled Issue Forms are available only to invited collaborators.
