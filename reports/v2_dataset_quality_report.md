## Interpretation and limitations

The sample remains concentrated in three publishers and five publication dates.
Publisher counts are source-composition diagnostics, not credibility rankings.
The absence of cross-source verification is shown explicitly. Publication dates
are used on the timeline where a distinct calendar event date was not supported.
The low quantity coverage and zero menu/business-format coverage are retained as
research limitations rather than filled by guesswork.

This enrichment used the short source contexts already reviewed with each record.
It made no network requests, LLM/VLM calls or new real-world claims. Previous
record versions were stored in the private history archive before migration.
