# Code of conduct

Participate with care, factual precision and respect. The project exists to organize public-source evidence for research.

No harassment, threats, discrimination, communal/religious/caste/ethnic interpretation, political targeting, retaliation, doxxing, boycott campaigns or reputational attacks are permitted. Do not speculate about personal identity or infer social groups from business names, cuisine or neighbourhoods.

Do not post unsupported allegations or unnecessary personal information. Critique sources, methods and specific fields with supporting public evidence. Inclusion does not establish wrongdoing.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate submissions, suspend records, restrict participation or close discussions. Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer during private evaluation; do not republish harmful content in a public report. No guaranteed response time is promised. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented and consistent with privacy needs.
